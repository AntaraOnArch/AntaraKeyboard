Word suggestion dictionaries

The word lists in this app are derived from FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave
(https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords), which builds frequency lists
from the OpenSubtitles 2018 corpus (http://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles2018.php).

They are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International License (CC BY-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode

Changes made for this app: words lowercased, entries that are not words removed,
lists limited to about 50,000 words per language, Serbian Latin and Cyrillic
entries merged and transliterated into both scripts.

The adapted word lists are shared under the same license (CC BY-SA 4.0).
